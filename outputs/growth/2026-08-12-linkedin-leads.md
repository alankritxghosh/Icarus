Zutti. Together they cover developer infrastructure, secure AI-native
platforms, developer experience, and a smaller engineering team. That gives a
more diagnostic mix than selecting the four largest audiences.

For each, collect before drafting:

1. One post from the last 30 days that contains a personal technical opinion.
2. Confirmation that the headline role is still current on the full profile.
3. Whether they own developer productivity, platform engineering, onboarding,
   engineering knowledge continuity, or agent adoption.
4. A single ask: technical critique, one-repository trial, or introduction.

Do not manufacture personalization from the headline alone and do not send a
connection note in the same pass as profile verification.
