# Reply voice — read from the real record

Source: `x.com/alankritxghosh/with_replies`, read 2026-08-22. This is a
DIFFERENT register from his posts, and the earlier `replies-queue.md` payloads
were written in the wrong one — they read like posts pasted into a reply box.

## What he actually does in replies

| Reply | Views |
|---|---|

**The mechanics:**
- **Very short.** Usually a fragment, rarely a full paragraph. He answers the
  literal question and stops.
- **No preamble.** No "great post", no "love this", no restating the question.
- **No emoji, no hashtags.** Consistent with his posts.
- **Casual punctuation** — "lets connect", no apostrophe. Don't over-polish him.
- **One exception:** when he holds a real position he writes a long,
  comma-spliced, thinking-out-loud sentence ("Marketing is not a necessary evil,
  its more of a preference, I am someone who enjoys building things…"). That is
  his unguarded voice and it is fine to use when the reply carries an argument.

## What the record also shows about strategy

**Almost every reply so far is networking, not substance** — "lets connect"
under "drop a hi" threads. That is what built the 13 followers, and it is
exactly the build-in-public reciprocal cohort `audience.md` says is not the
audience. **The reply mechanism is already in use; it is aimed at the wrong
thing.**

**The 85-view reply proves the mechanism works.** It sat under a 44K-view post.
Reply reach is a function of the parent's reach, not of his follower count —
which is the whole reason Play A can work at 13 followers.

## Drafting rules for replies (supersede `replies-queue.md` length)

1. **2–4 lines, under ~250 characters.** Shorter than a post, always.
2. **Open on the substance, not on the person.** No "great thread".
3. **One number, one sentence of consequence.** Do not stack three findings.
4. **Never pitch. Never link.** The profile is the pitch; that is what the bio
   rewrite was for.
5. **Add, don't agree.** A reply that only agrees is a like with extra steps.
