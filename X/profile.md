## The Premium question — a real decision, not a growth hack

X advertises **boosted replies** and **post analytics** as Premium features.
Both sit directly on top of this plan:

- The entire Play A bet is that replies carry us. If unverified replies rank
  lower in threads, we are running our one lever at a handicap.
- Post analytics is what closes Q1 (profile visits, link clicks, follower vs
  non-follower impressions) — the measurement H10 needs.

**Honest limits:** the reply boost is X's own marketing claim, not something we
have measured, and the ranking effect size is unknown. It also buys a checkmark,
which on a technical account is neutral at best.

**The call is Alankrit's.** The case for: it is cheap, and it targets the exact
bottleneck plus the exact measurement gap in one purchase. The case against: it
is paying for distribution before proving the content earns any — and if E1
works unverified, the result is cleaner and cheaper. **A defensible sequence is:
run E1 unverified for two weeks, then buy it and compare.** That also makes the
purchase an experiment rather than a hope.

## The job, in one sentence

A stranger arrives from a reply, having read one paragraph of yours. They spend
~3 seconds deciding whether you will be worth seeing again. The bio has to
answer: **what does he do, and why is he worth following rather than liking.**

Most technical bios answer only the first. The second is what earns a follow.

## Bio — recommended (147 chars)

> Building Icarus: engineering memory for coding agents. It cites the PR the
> answer came from, or says no one wrote it down. I post the measurements.

**Why each part:**
- *"engineering memory for coding agents"* — the category, in the words the
  primary segment uses. Not "AI for developers".
- *"cites the PR... or says no one wrote it down"* — the differentiator, stated
  as behaviour rather than as a claim. It is also the only half a competitor
  can't copy cheaply.
- *"I post the measurements."* — **this is the follow reason.** It tells them
  what they get by staying, and it is true: 16 experiment records back it.

## Alternates, if the above reads wrong to you
