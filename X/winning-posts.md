# Winning posts — the teardowns

**One home rule:** the post text, its metrics, and its verification note live in
`~/Documents/Obsidian Vault/Icarus/X Content.md` § Posted. That file is
authoritative. This file holds only the **teardown** — the mechanism, why we
think it worked, and the transferable lesson. Never copy metrics here.

Entry bar: a post enters when it is in the top third of the account by reach
**or** drew a reply. Both criteria are weak at this sample size; revisit at n=30.

---

### 2026-01-22 · "Working alone feels like freedom" — highest reach in the record
**Mechanism:** two lines, a belief violation, no product, no link, no ask.
**Why we think it worked:** it cost the reader nothing and resolved completely
inside the post. Also: **it is the least crafted post in the record and the
highest performing**, which is the origin of `hypotheses.md` H2.
**Transferable?** The *format* yes; the *substance* is an aphorism with no number
behind it, which every other rule in this system forbids. **Do not treat this as
a licence to write aphorisms.** Treat it as evidence that reach on this account
is not currently being decided by craft.
**Caution:** likely reached a peer/friend graph, not the primary segment (H8).
A 178-view post landing on non-engineers is worth less than a 40-view post
landing on agent builders.

### 2026-02-21 · First macOS app · 2026-02-22 · Two apps in a month
**Mechanism:** milestone + honest ask; specificity carrying a vulnerable line.
**Why we think they worked:** both drew the **only two replies in the account's
history**. Concrete stack, concrete timeline, an admission stated flatly with no
hedge before and no apology after.
**Transferable?** Partly. The register transfers. The vulnerability does not —
the no-failure-disclosure rule (Decision History, 2026-08-17) closes that route
deliberately, with the cost acknowledged. `hypotheses.md` H4 measures the price.

### 2026-08-13 · "Code shows what exists. Not why it exists." — best Icarus post
**Mechanism:** contrast pair. Belief violation in one breath.
**Why we think it worked:** it opens on the reader's own experience, not on the
product. The product arrives after the reader already believes in the problem.
**Transferable?** **Yes — this is the account's core reproducible move.**
Generator in `hooks.md` §1.

### 2026-08-15 · The rejected patch — best-written post in the record
**Mechanism:** scene opener → mechanism → product. Three beats, ~40 words.
**Why it is here despite 22 views:** it is the cleanest execution of the
structural template and the resolution order is right — product third, as the
answer to a problem the reader now has. Its reach is evidence for H2, not
evidence against the structure.
**Transferable?** Yes. This is the template in `voice.md` § The structural
template. Reuse it.

### 2026-08-23 · "a pull request nobody merged" — best post since February
**Mechanism:** question put to the reader → flat one-line self-answer ("I don't.")
→ the mechanism they can check against their own week. Product never named.
**Why we think it worked:** it is the Aug 13 contrast pair with the product
removed entirely. The reader is not asked to care about Icarus to find the
sentence true — the unmerged-PR fact stands on its own, so there is nothing to
discount. **No link card**, which the two highest-reach posts in the record also
lack (`hypotheses.md` H7).
**Why this entry outranks the others:** 44 views is 5x the Aug 17–18 median, and
**2 likes is the first time any post has moved past 1**. `analytics.md` treats
likes as a Tier 3 vanity metric *because they had no variance across 13–178
views*. That justification no longer holds; the metric is worth re-reading.
**Transferable?** **Yes, and it is now the strongest version of the core move.**
Aug 13 opened on a contrast and closed on the product; this opens on the reader
and never arrives at the product at all — and beat it on engagement with a
quarter of the link-card reach. The question worth testing next is whether
*naming Icarus at all* is what suppressed the ten that failed.
**Caution:** n=1, and it was found by hand three days late rather than captured,
so a slice of the reach may be from Alankrit's own revisits.

## What the wins have in common

1. Not one of them opens on "I built".
2. The two highest carry no product and no link.
3. The best-performing product post opens on a contrast the reader already
   half-believes.
4. **Reach and craft are uncorrelated across this sample.** Any lesson drawn from
   reach alone is currently unsafe.
