# Audience — who specifically should care

Grounded in `outputs/growth/2026-08-12-distribution-plan.md` and
`outputs/growth/2026-08-12-x-outreach-audit.md`. Where a claim is inference
rather than evidence, it says so.

## Primary: the coding-agent power user

Ships with Claude Code / Cursor / Codex daily. Has personally watched an agent
confidently rebuild something, or "fix" a bug that was already fixed. Reads
arXiv abstracts. Has opinions about context windows and MCP.

**Why they care:** the failure Icarus addresses is one they have *felt*, and
they lack the vocabulary for it. "A merged PR leaves a commit, a refused one
leaves nothing" is a sentence that names their experience.

**What converts them:** a mechanism they can reuse tomorrow. Not a product.

**Where they are:** replies under agent-tooling posts; arXiv-adjacent AI
research accounts; MCP and Claude Code community threads.

## Secondary: the developer-tool builder

Building agent infrastructure, retrieval, or devtools. Cares about evals,
retrieval quality, honesty guarantees, and how you *measure* an agent.

**Why they care:** we have measurement material they don't — 0/11 → 1 of 4
independent sessions on tool descriptions (4/4 retired 2026-08-25, see the vault's
Agent Mode note — do not restate it), the anti-correlated attribution detector,
the description-recall board. These are results, not opinions.

**What converts them:** methodology. The negative results are the strongest
currency here, and the no-failure-disclosure rule does not block them — a
deleted detector is a diagnosis we made, not a shortfall we confess.

## Tertiary: the engineering leader (10–200 engineers)

Head of engineering, platform/DX lead, CTO, technical founder. Owns onboarding
time and the cost of context loss between people.

**Why they care:** budget lives here. But X is a weak channel for them —
`outputs/growth/2026-08-12-linkedin-leads.md` puts this cohort on LinkedIn
deliberately.

**Treat as:** a beneficiary of X reach, not a target of X content. Do not dilute
the feed for them.

## Explicitly not the audience

- Generic "AI founder" / build-in-public accounts. High engagement, zero
  conversion, and their register is exactly what we are avoiding.
- Non-technical startup Twitter.
- Job-seeking developers.

### The consequence that matters most

The account has ranged 5–178 views on ~13 followers. **Reach here is therefore
almost entirely off-graph** — X surfacing posts to non-followers — and the
follower graph is a rounding error in the view count either way. Two readings
follow, both actionable:

1. **Off-graph surfacing works on this account** (178 views proves it), and it
   surfaced personal/career posts far more than technical ones. Whether that is
   topic, recency, or account age is unknown.
2. **Every single follower is ~8% of the graph.** At 13, follower growth stops
   being a vanity metric and becomes the highest-leverage number available —
   and *which* 13 becomes more important than how many.
