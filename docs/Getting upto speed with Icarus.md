First user experience with Icarus
The first user experience with Icarus is supposed to be quite special, I envision it to be in the following manner:
A new user downloads Icarus, opens it and connects their github account to it via the Oauth in place, then they move into connecting their repository with Icarus and once the repo is connected, they are then added into a skippable onboarding session for the repository something that a senior engineer or their manager would do, they are brought to terms with the architecture, the most recent decisions verified and cited in the PR's and explained the tech stack, once the 30 minutes onboarding for the repo is done, then the user is free to look around and ask questions regarding the repository and the codebase to double down on their understanding after which within a few hours the user is well and truly equipped with making changes to the codebase and work alongside their team without any friction.
While Icarus, is completing the onboarding in the background it will also keep indexing the repo, so that the entire ingestion is completed seamlessly.
For users who are already on Icarus and have clocked out of it for say 2 or 3 weeks or anytime span, when they clock back into their repository they are greeted with a summary of sort giving them context on all the decisions and changes that were made when they were away from the repository so that they are contextually aware, the idea and the vision is to bring all members of the team to the same page as soon as possible, and making it possible for non engineering background individuals to take part in development even via vibe coding, by helping them understand random codebases or their company codebases without having to read through lines and lines of readme files or architecture docs. It also has to assume that people are short on attention span so it needs to grab their attention and bring them up to speed as quickly as possible.
