# Business — the sequence, and why it is this order

Started 2026-08-24. Nothing on ICP, pricing, unit economics, market or funding
had ever been written down before this date; CLAUDE.md has named business
decisions as the priority since 2026-07-16 without them being made.

**Each piece is blocked by the one above it.** Doing them out of order produces
a plan that has to be rewritten, which is the failure this ordering exists to
avoid.

| # | Piece | Blocked by | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ICP** | nothing | Everything else is a function of who buys. Pricing, positioning, GTM and the deck all derive from it |
| 2 | **Unit economics** | 1 | What one customer costs to serve. Needed before a price can be defended, and it is measurable from our own logs rather than guessed |
| 3 | **Pricing** | 1, 2 | Value to the ICP, floored by cost |
| 4 | **Trust and legal** | 1 | What a design partner must see before connecting a private repo. Blocks the first paying customer, not the tenth |
| 5 | **Competition and wedge** | 1 | `docs/COMPETITIVE.md` exists and predates the ICP; it needs re-reading against a named buyer |
| 6 | **Market size** | 1, 3 | Bottom-up only. A top-down number nobody can reconstruct is the kind of claim this company refuses everywhere else |
| 7 | **GTM** | 1-5 | Synthesised from what has actually been measured: outreach, X, HN, open source |
| 8 | **The plan and the Antler deck** | all | Assembled, not invented. If a slide cannot point at one of the above, it does not go in |

## The rule for all of it

**Every number traceable to a source, or stated as an assumption in the open.**
Investor material is where [[Decision History]]'s no-failure-disclosure rule
does NOT apply (see Work Queue section 10): a company whose one non-negotiable
is that it cannot bluff does not get to bluff in its own pitch.

Where a number needs external validation we do not have, it says so rather than
being filled in with something plausible.
